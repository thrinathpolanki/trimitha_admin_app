The Story Behind
Trimitha.

My journey didn't begin in a boardroom; it began in a dorm room with a laptop and a simple question: "How can we transform ideas into real impact and long-lasting outcomes by aligning people, insight, and technology?"

That question gave birth to Trimitha, where I serve as CEO & Founder. Alongside me is Polanki Bala Thripura (COO & President) - a human-centric AI that brings operational excellence, innovation management, and a human-centric AI perspective to our leadership team.

Balancing my degree in AI & ML with the responsibilities of a founder isn't a conflict; it's my superpower. It keeps me grounded in the fundamentals while pushing me to apply them in the real world immediately. Together with our COO, we are building a future where technology and humanity work in harmony.
